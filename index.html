Services
What's included in a general service?
Cleaning of air filters, front covers, coils, blower wheel and drain tray. We check drainage flow and inspect for visible faults. Takes about 30–45 min per unit.
Chemical wash vs chemical overhaul?
Chemical wash is done in-place — no dismantling. Chemical overhaul means the full unit is removed, every part taken apart, cleaned individually and reassembled. Overhaul is for cases where washing can't fix the problem.
Do you service all brands?
Yes — Daikin, Mitsubishi, Panasonic, LG, Samsung, Fujitsu, Toshiba, Midea and others. For Cassette or ceiling units, mention it when booking as extra time may be needed.
How long does each service take?
General: ~30 min/unit. FoamJet: ~40 min/unit. Chemical wash: ~45 min/unit. Chemical overhaul: ~60–90 min/unit.
Do I need to be home?
Yes. An adult must be present for the full duration to provide access and sign off on the job.
Pricing & payment
Are there hidden charges?
None. All prices include GST and transport. Additional work is always quoted and confirmed before proceeding — you are never charged for work you didn't agree to.
What payment methods?
Cash, PayNow and bank transfer. Payment on day of service. Annual contracts paid upfront.
Why is the repair diagnostic $60?
It covers the technician's time and transport. If you proceed with the repair, the $60 is waived — you only pay the repair cost.
Bookings
How quickly can I get an appointment?
Same-day or next-day slots available. Book by 12pm for a same-day afternoon slot. Weekends fill fast — book 2 days ahead.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Cancel or reschedule 24+ hours before at no charge. Within 24 hours or no-show = $20 admin fee. Annual contract holders get one free reschedule per year.
What areas do you serve?
Singapore island-wide — all HDB, condo and landed. Commercial on request.
After service
Is there a service guarantee?
Yes — 30-day guarantee on every job. Same issue recurs, we return at no charge. Annual contract holders get priority re-service within 48 hours.
Still not cold after servicing?
Contact us within 30 days — we'll investigate at no cost. Common causes are low refrigerant gas or a faulty component, both covered under our follow-up policy.
How often should I service my aircon?
Every 3 months is ideal in Singapore's climate. Minimum twice a year. Annual chemical wash once a year prevents mould and maintains air quality.
