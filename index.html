Frequently Asked Questions
Services
What's included in a general service?
Cleaning of air filters, front covers, coils, blower wheel and drain tray. We check drainage flow and inspect for visible faults. Takes about 30–45 min per unit.
Chemical wash vs chemical overhaul?
Chemical wash is done in-place — chemicals applied without dismantling the unit. Chemical overhaul means the full unit is removed from the wall, every part taken apart, cleaned individually and reassembled. Overhaul is needed only when washing alone can't fix the issue.
Do you service all aircon brands?
Yes — Daikin, Mitsubishi, Panasonic, LG, Samsung, Fujitsu, Toshiba, Midea and others. For Cassette and ceiling-mounted units, please mention when booking as extra time may be needed.
How long does each service take?
General: ~30 min/unit. FoamJet: ~40 min/unit. Chemical wash: ~45 min/unit. Chemical overhaul: ~60–90 min/unit.
Do I need to be home?
Yes. An adult must be present for the full duration to provide access and sign off on the job.
Can a dirty aircon affect my health?
Yes. Neglected units accumulate dust, mould and bacteria. When running, these circulate into your indoor air — triggering allergies, asthma and respiratory issues. Regular servicing and an annual chemical wash are essential.

After Service
Is there a service guarantee?
Yes — 30-day guarantee on all jobs. Same issue recurs, we return at no charge. Annual contract holders get priority re-service within 48 hours.
Still not cold after servicing?
Contact us within 30 days — we'll investigate at no cost. Common causes are low refrigerant gas or a faulty component, both covered under our follow-up policy.
How often should I service my aircon?
NEA recommends every 3 months. Minimum twice a year. Annual chemical wash prevents mould and keeps air quality healthy for your family.
