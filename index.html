Frequently Asked Questions
Services
What's included in a general service?
Cleaning of air filters, front covers, coils, blower wheel and drain tray. We also check drainage flow and inspect for visible faults. Takes about 30–45 min per unit.
Chemical wash vs chemical overhaul — what's the difference?
Chemical wash is done in-place — chemicals applied to the coil without dismantling. Chemical overhaul means the full indoor unit is removed from the wall, every part taken apart, cleaned individually and reassembled. Overhaul is needed only when washing alone cannot fix the issue.
Do you service all aircon brands?
Yes — Daikin, Mitsubishi, Panasonic, LG, Samsung, Fujitsu, Toshiba, Midea and others. For Cassette and ceiling-mounted units, please mention when booking as extra time may be needed.
How long does each service take?
General: ~30 min/unit. FoamJet: ~40 min/unit. Chemical wash: ~45 min/unit. Chemical overhaul: ~60–90 min/unit. We confirm estimated duration when the booking is confirmed.
Do I need to be home?
Yes. An adult must be present for the full duration to provide access and sign off on the job. We cannot service units if no one is home.
Can a dirty aircon affect my health?
Yes. Neglected units accumulate dust, mould and bacteria. When running, these are circulated into your indoor air — triggering allergies, asthma, and respiratory issues. Regular servicing and an annual chemical wash are essential for healthy air quality.
Pricing & Payment
Are there hidden charges?
None. All prices include GST and transport. If additional work is needed, the technician quotes you before proceeding. You are never charged for work you didn't agree to.
What payment methods do you accept?
Cash, PayNow and bank transfer. Payment is due on the day of service after completion. Annual contracts are paid upfront to lock in pricing.
Why is the repair diagnostic $60?
It covers the technician's time and transport to diagnose the issue. If you proceed with the repair, the $60 is fully waived — you only pay for the repair itself.

Same-day or next-day slots available subject to technician availability. Book by 12pm for a same-day afternoon slot. Weekend slots fill fast — book at least 2 days ahead.
Can I reschedule or cancel?
Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before your appointment at no charge. Within 24 hours or no-show = $20 admin fee. Annual contract holders get one free reschedule per year.
What areas do you serve?
Singapore island-wide — all HDB, condo and landed residential. Commercial and office units available on request — contact us for a quote.
After Service
Is there a service guarantee?
Yes — 30-day guarantee on all jobs. Same issue recurs within 30 days, we return at no charge. Annual contract holders get priority re-service within 48 hours.
Still not cold after servicing?
Contact us within 30 days and we'll investigate at no cost. Common causes are low refrigerant gas or a faulty component — both covered under our follow-up visit policy.
How often should I service my aircon?
NEA recommends every 3 months. At minimum, twice a year. Annual chemical wash once a year prevents mould buildup and maintains healthy air quality for your family.
